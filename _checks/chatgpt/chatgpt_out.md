This study tested Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on 300 coding problems each, drawn from MBPP+, HumanEval+, and designed trap variants. Agents reported their confidence before receiving three increasingly forceful rebuttals, with code executed after every turn. Confidence predicted whether correct answers survived challenges (AUROC 0.78 for Haiku, 0.81 for Sonnet), but not whether incorrect answers were repaired.

I run livenerf, a pre-registered benchmark that tests Claude Opus 5.5 against a locked panel of 78 questions every day to measure whether a frontier model gets worse after launch. The benchmark has run for 62 consecutive days without missing a day, with no change detected across five 10-day windows. The project has about 2,000 GitHub stars and a public Discord with over 270 members discussing the daily results. In November 2026, I also entered two Apart Research sprints independently, studying the oversight of AI agents.
