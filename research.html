livenerf: does a frontier model get quietly worse after launch?

Nathan Langley

Pre-registered daily benchmark series · 1,900+ GitHub stars · run every day since September 24, 2026, none missed

A daily, append-only benchmark that tests the "model nerf" claim. A locked panel of 78 GPQA, MMLU-Pro, competition-math and AIME questions, chosen because the model only sometimes gets them right, is rerun every day against a 10-day launch baseline. The design and the detectable effect size were published before the results.

Hold 'Em or Fold 'Em: does a coding agent's confidence tell an overseer which claims survive a challenge?

Nathan Langley

Apart Research AI x Epistemics sprint, November 2026 · solo entry, Track 1

This study tested Claude Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on 300 coding problems each, drawn from MBPP+, HumanEval+, and designed trap variants. Agents reported their confidence before receiving three increasingly forceful rebuttals, with code executed after every turn. Confidence predicted whether correct answers survived challenges (AUROC 0.78 for Haiku, 0.81 for Sonnet), but did not detectably predict whether incorrect answers were repaired.
